## Keywords

Use the English term in **Keywords** lines. The Indonesian term is what the
thesis text uses.

| Keyword | Bahasa Indonesia | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| acceleration | percepatan | Peak acceleration of the shaker table, in m/s² or g |
| amplitude | amplitudo | How far the shaker table moves from its centre, in cm |
| Atterberg limits | batas Atterberg | The water contents where clay turns from brittle to plastic (plastic limit, PL) and from plastic to liquid (liquid limit, LL) |
| consolidation | konsolidasi | Squeezing water out of a soil under a steady load, so it becomes denser and stiffer |
| cyclic softening | pelunakan siklik | Clay losing stiffness and strength step by step under repeated shaking |
| cycles | jumlah siklus | Number of back-and-forth shaking motions applied |
| decimal separator | pemisah desimal | The mark between the whole and decimal part of a number: a comma in Indonesian (0,5), a point in English (0.5) |
| drainage | drainase | Whether pore water can escape during shaking (drained) or not (undrained) |
| filler phrase | frasa pengisi | A phrase that adds words but no meaning, e.g. "Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa" |
| italics | huruf miring | Slanted type. In Indonesian text it marks foreign terms, e.g. *shear wave* |
| KBBI | KBBI | Kamus Besar Bahasa Indonesia, the official dictionary. It decides the standard (baku) spelling |
| liquefaction | likuefaksi | Saturated sand losing nearly all strength because pore water pressure rises and pushes grains apart |
| plasticity index | indeks plastisitas (PI) | LL minus PL. It decides whether a soil behaves more like sand or like clay |
| pore water pressure | tekanan air pori | Pressure of the water between soil grains |
| relative density | densitas relatif | How packed a soil is, between its loosest (0%) and densest (100%) state |
| repeatability | pengulangan | Running the same test more than once to see how much results scatter |
| saturation | kejenuhan | How fully the spaces between grains are filled with water |
| shaker table | meja getar | The table that shakes the sample to imitate an earthquake |
| stiffness | kekakuan (modulus geser, G) | Resistance to shearing. G = ρVs², so stiffness follows Vs |
| Vs | kecepatan gelombang geser | Speed of the S-wave through the sample, from travel time over distance |
