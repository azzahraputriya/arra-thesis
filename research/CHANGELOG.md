## 2026-11-07 · Start the research trail (#4)

Compared the two earlier sand theses (S1, S2) and set the direction for a
clay thesis. The key result is that both theses' accelerations are computed
from frequency, not measured, and both detect liquefaction by eye. That shapes
what a clay thesis should measure instead.
