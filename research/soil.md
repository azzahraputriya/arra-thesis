# Soil

The clay itself: where it comes from, what kind it is, and how samples are
prepared.

## Proposals

### P2 · Make plasticity the main variable

- **Keywords:** plasticity index, Atterberg limits, prior work
- Measure the Atterberg limits, then test clay mixed with 0/20/40% sand.
  S2's Garut sand serves as the 0% clay end, so the thesis reads as a
  sand-to-clay series.

### P5 · Control the clay's state by consolidation

- **Keywords:** consolidation, relative density
- Squeeze the clay under known loads instead of pouring and tapping it to a
  density. This gives a controlled, repeatable starting state (fixes the
  ad-hoc density scale in F7).

### Q3 · Atterberg limits in-house?

- **Keywords:** Atterberg limits, rig
- Likely yes. The standard test (ASTM D4318) needs: a Casagrande cup with a
  grooving tool for the liquid limit (or a fall cone instead), a glass plate
  for rolling 3 mm threads for the plastic limit, a 0.425 mm (#40) sieve, a
  drying oven at about 110 °C, and a balance reading to 0.01 g. The oven and
  the cup are the parts to check for.
