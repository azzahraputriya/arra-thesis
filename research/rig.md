## Findings

### F6 · The rig as S1 and S2 describe it

- **Keywords:** shaker table, amplitude
- Acrylic box, 7 × 20 × 30 cm, 8 mm walls, rated to 2 bar, with a water inlet
  at the bottom and an outlet port for pressure. S-wave transmitter and
  receiver on opposite sides, driven by a sonic viewer (12 V battery) and
  read on an oscilloscope. Shaker table with horizontal circular motion, up
  to 5 cm amplitude, with an accelerometer. Sample mass: 4.5 kg (S1),
  3 kg (S2).
- **Source:** S1 III.3.1–III.3.2, S2 III.3.1–III.3.2.
